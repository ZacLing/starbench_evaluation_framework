# Workflow

## Step 1: Classify By Work Type

Choose the relevant work types before writing:

- `task_framing`: Frame The Deliverable At The Right Level
- `platform_boundary`: Define Platform Boundary And Interaction Model
- `derived_variable_contract`: Define Derived Research Variable Contract
- `data_governance`: Govern Data Lineage, Timing, Missingness, And Comparability
- `validation_design`: Separate Exploration, Confirmation, And Robustness
- `selection_control`: Control Outcome-driven Selection
- `cost_capacity`: Make Feasibility Survive Costs And Capacity
- `monitoring_failure_gates`: Define Monitoring, Escalation, Pause, And Exit Gates
- `research_production_consistency`: Preserve Research-to-production Consistency
- `compliance_audit`: Design Audit, Compliance, And External Evidence Packages
- `roadmap_resources`: Phase Work With Resources And Stop Points
- `success_exit`: Define Success, Downgrade, Pause, And Exit Criteria

## Step 2: Establish Senior Framing

- Name the decision the deliverable enables.
- Define the system, platform, method, or governance boundary.
- Identify what would make the output invalid, misleading, non-actionable, or non-promotable.

## Step 3: Convert Expert Judgment Into Deliverable Evidence

- Use atomic cards to create paragraphs, tables, thresholds, artifacts, owners, and escalation rules.
- Keep domain examples proportional and only when they match the prompt.

## Step 4: Check For Overfitting

- Remove unnecessary task-specific terms from the main argument.
- Keep specific terms in examples, specializations, or appendices.
