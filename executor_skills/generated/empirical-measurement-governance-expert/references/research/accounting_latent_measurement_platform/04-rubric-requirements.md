# Rubric-derived Requirements

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable use Chinese structured Markdown with second-level section headings?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable include all fourteen required sections from executive summary through conclusion?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable avoid writing code, assuming local data files already exist, or reproducing a specific paper result?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable position the platform as a method evaluation and governance tool rather than an automated replacement for theory-based variable definition?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable distinguish proxy measurement-method comparison from structural-model robustness analysis?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable set clear exclusion or escalation gatekeeping rules that exclude leakage, method inapplicability, severe sample bias, or catastrophic simulation failures before considering theory fit?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable include both reflective and formative simulation data-generating processes with the correct causal direction for each?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable require a unified sample and unified missing-data handling rule for the primary empirical comparison, with method-specific missingness handled only as sensitivity analysis?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable distinguish PLS-SEM from PCA, OLS two-stage proxy construction, and SEM or CFA, and require implementation validation to prevent mislabeled PLS-SEM?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable define dependent-variable leakage broadly enough to include direct variables, lags, transformations, concurrent proxies, factor scores, lineage, timestamps, and semantic review?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable require all evaluated method results, including failed or insignificant ones, to appear in the final report unless an approved exclusion is recorded?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable define a hard default rule that conflicting signs among otherwise valid methods make the conclusion not robust unless a predefined governance approval or escalation rule permits a specific main method with full disclosure?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable identify at least ten concrete method, simulation, empirical-data, governance, interpretation, software, and reviewer-acceptance risks with corresponding mitigations?

- fail_fast=True: Does the implementation roadmap phase the platform from an MVP through method expansion, empirical data, PLS or SEM governance, reporting automation, and governance adoption with deliverables, teams, success criteria, and publication-use permissions for each phase?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable estimate long-term maintenance, software-version, method-library, audit-storage, governance-review, and formative-simulation costs with realistic ranges or cost levels?

- fail_fast=True: Does the deliverable define quantitative success standards and exit or pause conditions, including platform-level stopping thresholds and method-specific pause triggers?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable define the platform's physical form and user interaction model with realistic options and tradeoffs?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable specify an immutable technical pre-registration mechanism with timestamps, hashes or version IDs, frozen configurations, and amendment logs?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable require pre-registration rationales and exploratory-analysis disclosure so unsupported pre-registration choices can be downgraded or rejected?

- fail_fast=False: Does the simulation design vary loading, noise, sample size, structural coefficient, dimensional correlation, and heterogeneous inter-indicator correlation rather than relying on one Monte Carlo setting?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable define fair indicator-set rules, including how single-indicator methods may select one indicator from the same candidate list and how alternative indicator sets become separate scenarios?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable substantively compare single indicator, equal-weight index, rank or decile index, factor or PCA, PLS or PLS-SEM, SEM or CFA, and extensible methods across key dimensions such as suitability, sample and indicator-quality requirements, strengths, failure modes, and recommended roles?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable require method-applicability pre-checks and mark methods as not applicable or not comparable when diagnostics fail?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable explain PLS-SEM overfitting or implicit dependent-variable information risk and require comparison against methods that do not use the dependent variable in measurement construction?

- fail_fast=False: When the research involves panel data, does the deliverable address panel-data measurement stability, such as annual loading stability and performance differences under fixed-effects settings?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable provide at least three quantitative success metrics and substantively cover core evaluation dimensions such as bias, variance, MSE, tail failure, and coefficient recovery rather than reporting only average error?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable treat benchmark proxies as noisy comparators and recommend pre-registering benchmark choices or using multiple independent benchmarks or external events for triangulation while disclosing caveats?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable define research outputs and audit artifacts that support internal review, paper writing, reviewer response, reproducibility, and reviewer-friendly appendices?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable include substantive audit-data compliance controls, such as data minimization, identifier anonymization or irreversible processing, role-based access control, retention periods, and internal versus external audit package distinctions?

- fail_fast=False: Does the executive summary recommend whether to build the platform, name a minimum viable starting scenario, and warn that one simulation cannot prove one method is always best?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable require pre-registration exploration to be recorded in an exploration log and automatically downgrade the evaluation to exploratory when unrecorded pre-registration analyses are discovered and conflict with the final registered parameters?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable require formative simulation scenarios to use an indicators-to-latent-construct generation direction and prohibit common-factor generation for formative data?

- fail_fast=False: Does the deliverable require complete disclosure of all executed method results, including insignificant and failed results, and include an automated detection mechanism for selective reporting?
