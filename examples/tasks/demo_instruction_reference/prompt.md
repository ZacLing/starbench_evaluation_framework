# Task: Urban Heat Resilience Memo

Write a policy memo for a mid-sized city deciding how to reduce neighborhood heat risk over the next three summers.

Create exactly one primary deliverable:

```text
./outputs/urban_heat_resilience_memo.md
```

Requirements:

- Write between 700 and 1000 words.
- Use the exact title `Three-Summer Urban Heat Resilience Plan`.
- Include these exact section headings:
  - `Executive Summary`
  - `Risk Prioritization`
  - `Interventions`
  - `Measurement Plan`
  - `Equity and Implementation Risks`
- Include exactly three priority neighborhoods named `Riverside`, `East Market`, and `South Junction`.
- Include at least four interventions, including tree canopy, cool roofs, shade structures, and cooling centers.
- Include at least five measurable metrics, including land surface temperature, emergency calls, cooling center visits, tree survival rate, and resident-reported heat stress.
- Include a simple timeline with Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 actions.
- Do not claim that tree planting alone solves heat risk.

When finished, briefly report the output path and the word count.
